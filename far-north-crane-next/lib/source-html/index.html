Frequently Asked Questions

How long can I rent a crane for my commercial or residential project?

Our crane rental services are flexible and can be tailored to your project's duration. Whether you need a crane for a few hours, days, weeks, or months, we can accommodate your rental needs. Our team will work closely with you to determine the optimal rental duration and provide competitive pricing accordingly.

Are your crane rentals accompanied by experienced operators?

Yes, we have a team of skilled and certified operators who have extensive experience in operating cranes in commercial and residential environments. Our operators are trained to prioritize safety, efficiency, and adherence to industry regulations. They will ensure that your lifting or maintenance operations in commercial or residential spaces are carried out smoothly and securely.

What safety measures do you have in place for crane rentals?

Safety is our top priority. Far North Crane never fails to meet the safety protocols in commercial and residential areas. Our cranes undergo regular maintenance and inspections to ensure they are in optimal working condition. Our operators are well-versed in safety procedures and follow industry best practices when it comes lifting or transporting heavy equipment.

Do you provide on-site assessments before renting out a crane?

Yes, we prioritize safety and believe in delivering tailored solutions to our clients. Before renting out a crane for your we perform on-site assessments at your residential or commercial project to evaluate factors such as site accessibility, terrain conditions, and specific lifting requirements. This assessment helps us recommend a comprehensive plan for your project.
