Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any specific permits or regulatory requirements I need to address when renting a crane for a commercial or residential project?

Yes, operating cranes often requires permits and adherence to safety regulations. The kind of permits and permission you will need depend on your location and project type. Therefore, it is crucial to check with local authorities and the crane rental industry to ensure compliance with all necessary permits and safety standards.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Far North Crane is fully licensed, certified and insured. We take every measure to ensure the safety and protection before commercial and residential crane rentals service. Get in touch with us to discuss your project.

How far in advance should I book a crane rental for my commercial or residential project, and what is the scheduling process like?

It's advisable to book a crane well in advance to secure availability, especially during peak construction seasons. The exact lead time depends on factors such as crane type, location, and demand. The scheduling process typically involves contacting us, discussing your project requirements, obtaining a quote, and confirming dates and logistics to ensure a smooth and timely crane rental experience.

How is the cost of crane rental determined, and what factors influence the pricing?

The cost of crane rental depends on several factors, including the type of crane(rough terrain crane, mobile cranes or telescopic cranes) rental duration, maximum lift capacity and project location. Additionally, any special rigging equipment or accessories required will impact the price. Far North Crane believes in complete transparency and provides accurate estimated costs before beginning the project without any hidden cost surprises. based on your specific project details.

What safety measures are in place during crane rental services for commercial and residential projects?

We prioritize safety and typically provide trained and certified crane operators. We also offer insurance coverage for the crane and operator.
